# Footer trust plan and client demonstration

The footer should help a prospective client decide whether to contact Christina. Preserve the existing ivory, ink and brass design, with a quiet evidence row above the footer navigation. Mobile stacks the evidence into readable lines; no extra carousel, auto animation, or decorative award seals.

## Recommended content, in priority order

| Signal | Proposed treatment | Evidence needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Arizona aesthetician license | “Arizona licensed aesthetician · License #[number] · Verify license” | Full licensed name, exact license number/type and current status. Verify through the Arizona Barbering & Cosmetology Board. An aesthetician license does not itself establish authorization or specialist certification for every listed treatment. |
| Experience | “Practicing since [year]” or “8+ years in [accurate discipline]” | Confirm start date and scope. Do not count general beauty experience as permanent makeup experience unless accurate. |
| Google reviews | Compact linked “5.0 · 26 Google reviews” | Existing supplied rating and count; recheck before launch of the footer. Values are static, not a live feed. |
| Specialist training | One concise line naming relevant permanent makeup or restorative tattoo training | Exact course, training provider, credential and date. Distinguish training certificates from professional licenses and accreditation. |
| Studio and care | Confirmed address, Call/Text, Google directions; aftercare or consultation information when available | Address and phone already confirmed. Obtain actual aftercare and booking policies before adding them. |
| Chamber membership | Small linked member name or logo beneath primary evidence | Current membership and public directory listing. Membership is not professional accreditation. |
| Product brands | Optional separate “Products we use” line | Only brands genuinely used, accurate relationship and permitted brand assets. Do not imply endorsement or certification. |

## Suggested composition

1. Above the existing footer: license and verification link, confirmed experience, compact Google review link. Use readable text and restrained separators, not a wall of badge cards.
2. Keep the map and practical contact details close by, followed by real social accounts.
3. Place any verified training or chamber membership on a secondary line. Include brands only if they help clients assess treatments; omit if they crowd the footer.
4. Show no placeholders or unverified qualifications on the public page. The generated studio portrait is a concept and must not be treated as photographic evidence of the actual premises.
